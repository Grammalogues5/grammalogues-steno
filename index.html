5. Rule Book / Checking Rules
  • Exact word match: A correctly typed word is counted as correct.
  • Wrong word: A different word in the same position is counted as an error.
  • Omission: A word present in the master but missing from the transcription is counted as an omission.
  • Extra word: A word typed by the student that is not required by the master is counted as an extra word.
  • Punctuation: Punctuation is ignored for the basic word-accuracy calculation; the comparison is word-based.
  • Capitalization: Capital letters are ignored for the basic word-accuracy calculation.
  • Whitespace: Multiple spaces and line breaks are treated as normal word separators.
  • Timer: The 50-minute clock begins with the first typing activity after Start and stops at submission/finish or when time reaches zero.
