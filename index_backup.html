SSC Stenography Skill Test — Mistake Rules

1. FULL MISTAKES

  • Omission: Every omission of a word or figure. If a group of words is omitted, count as many mistakes as the actual number of omitted words.
  • Substitution: Every substitution of a wrong word or figure. For figures, the numeral or the word form is acceptable. The mistakes equal the dictated word(s) not written plus the wrongly substituted word(s).
  • Addition: Every added word, figure or symbol not occurring in the dictated passage.
  • Repetition: Repetition of a word.
  • Incomplete word: Every incomplete word.
  • Left-over words: All left-over words of the passage.
  • Abbreviation: Candidates must type the actual words as dictated. An abbreviation used for an expanded word, or vice versa, is a full mistake.
  • Capital letters: Words or lines typed in all CAPITAL letters are counted as full mistakes.

2. HALF MISTAKES

  • Wrong spelling.
  • Using singular for a plural noun and vice versa.
  • Omission of a full stop at the end of a sentence, or use of a full stop at the wrong place.
  • Using a small letter at the beginning of a sentence.
  • Non-capitalisation of proper nouns (name of a person, place, office, etc.).

SSC NOTES

  • Words that can be spelt/written in more than one acceptable form are not counted as errors. For example, the SSC guideline gives Honourable, Hon’ble, Hon., honourable and hon. as acceptable forms.
  • The guidelines are also applicable to Hindi Stenography Skill Test wherever applicable.

Method of Calculation

Percentage of Errors = (Full Mistakes + Half Mistakes / 2) × 100 ÷ Number of words in the Master Passage

The SSC denominator is selected according to the test speed and duration:

  • 800 words — 80 w.p.m. for 10 minutes
  • 1000 words — 100 w.p.m. for 10 minutes
  • 840 words — 120 w.p.m. for 7 minutes
  • 600 words — 120 w.p.m. for 5 minutes

Percentage is rounded to two decimal places.

Important: The website's automatic checker is an SSC-rules-based estimate. Some cases (especially context, acceptable alternative forms, incomplete words and proper nouns) can require human judgment. The SSC rules above are reproduced from the guideline you provided.
